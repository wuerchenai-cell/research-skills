# Verify: Derivation

## Purpose

Reconstruct a technical derivation and check it.

## Workflow

1. State the goal and known conditions.
2. List assumptions and constraints.
3. Reconstruct the derivation step by step.
4. Check units, dimensions, and limiting cases.
5. Provide a numeric example.

## Quality gates

- Each step is justified.
- Assumptions are explicit.
- Units and limiting cases are checked.

## Common pitfalls

- Skipping algebraic steps.
- Dropping boundary conditions.
