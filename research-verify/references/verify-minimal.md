# Verify: Minimal Validation

## Purpose

Propose a minimal test to confirm a technical claim.

## Workflow

1. State the claim and what would count as confirmation.
2. Propose the smallest measurable experiment or simulation.
3. List required inputs, expected output, and acceptance criteria.
4. Mark what remains unverified after the test.

## Quality gates

- The plan is minimal and measurable.
- Acceptance criteria are explicit.
- Remaining uncertainty is marked.

## Common pitfalls

- Proposing a full benchmark when a single measurement suffices.
- Omitting acceptance criteria.
