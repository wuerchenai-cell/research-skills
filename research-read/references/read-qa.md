# Read: Q&A

## Purpose

Answer questions about a document clearly and grounded in the original.

## Workflow

1. Identify the document and the question.
2. Locate the relevant section, page, equation, or figure.
3. Answer the question clearly.
4. Attach evidence to claims.
5. State assumptions and limitations.

## Quality gates

- The answer is grounded in the original document.
- Key claims are located with page numbers.
- Assumptions and limitations are explicit.
- The answer is verifiable.

## Common pitfalls

- Answering from memory instead of the document.
- Inventing page numbers or citations.
- Skipping evidence.
- Treating speculation as fact.
- Hiding uncertainty.
