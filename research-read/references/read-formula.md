# Read: Formula

## Purpose

Explain a formula clearly, with variables, meaning, derivation, and examples.

## Workflow

1. State the variables and their definitions.
2. Explain the physical or mathematical meaning.
3. Derive the formula step by step if needed.
4. Discuss assumptions and boundary conditions.
5. Provide a numeric example.

## Quality gates

- All variables are defined.
- The meaning is explained in plain language.
- The derivation is step by step.
- Assumptions and boundary conditions are explicit.
- The numeric example is verifiable.

## Common pitfalls

- Skipping variable definitions.
- Treating the formula as a black box.
- Hiding assumptions inside the derivation.
- Ignoring domain restrictions.
- Confusing necessary and sufficient conditions.
