# Read: Paper

## Purpose

Read a paper clearly and grounded in the original document.

## Workflow

1. Identify the paper and the user's question.
2. Verify identity: title, authors, venue, year, version.
3. Check for errata, corrections, or retractions.
4. Note the access level: full text, abstract only, or metadata only.
5. Extract text from the document.
6. Locate the relevant section, page, equation, or figure.
7. Explain the content clearly and grounded in the original.
8. Attach evidence to claims.

## Quality gates

- Identity is verified.
- Access level is stated.
- Key claims are located with page numbers.
- Formulas are explained with variable definitions.
- Figures are described with axes and labels.
- Assumptions and limitations are explicit.

## Common pitfalls

- Treating abstract as full text.
- Inventing page numbers or citations.
- Skipping identity and version checks.
- Confusing correlation with causation.
- Hiding assumptions inside explanations.
