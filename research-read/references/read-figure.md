# Read: Figure

## Purpose

Interpret a figure or table clearly, with axes, labels, trends, and limitations.

## Workflow

1. Describe what the figure shows.
2. Identify axes, labels, and legends.
3. Explain the trend or pattern.
4. Connect to the text and results.
5. Discuss limitations or alternative interpretations.

## Quality gates

- The figure type is identified.
- Axes and labels are described.
- Trends or patterns are explained.
- Connections to text and results are explicit.
- Limitations or alternative interpretations are discussed.

## Common pitfalls

- Treating the figure as self-explanatory.
- Skipping axes and labels.
- Confusing correlation with causation.
- Ignoring error bars or confidence intervals.
- Hiding limitations inside the description.
