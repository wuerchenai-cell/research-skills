# Compare: Setup

## Purpose

Extract each source's hypothesis, method, conditions, cost, and conclusion.

## Workflow

1. Define the comparison question and the fields to compare.
2. Extract each field from the original source, not from summaries.
3. Record conditions for every numeric or categorical claim.
4. Mark fields that are missing or unverified.

## Quality gates

- The comparison question is stated first.
- Every field has a source or is marked missing.
- Numeric claims carry their conditions.

## Common pitfalls

- Comparing numbers without recording conditions.
- Treating paper count or prestige as evidence.
