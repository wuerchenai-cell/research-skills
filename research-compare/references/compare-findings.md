# Compare: Findings

## Purpose

Explain why conclusions differ across sources.

## Workflow

1. Identify the conditions that explain the difference.
2. Explain the mechanism linking conditions to the conclusion.
3. Mark unresolved conflicts and what would resolve them.
4. State limitations of the comparison.

## Quality gates

- Differences are explained by conditions, not by aggregating.
- Unresolved conflicts are marked.
- Limitations are stated.

## Common pitfalls

- Declaring a winner without accounting for conditions.
- Treating incompatible settings as directly comparable.
