# Compare: Evidence Mapping

## Purpose

Trace each claim in the comparison back to its source.

## Workflow

1. For each claim, note source, page/section, and exact quote or paraphrase.
2. Separate source-stated facts from inferences.
3. Mark claims that need reopening the original.
4. Preserve contradictions instead of averaging.

## Quality gates

- Every claim has a source location.
- Facts and inferences are labeled.
- Contradictions are preserved, not resolved by vote.

## Common pitfalls

- Mixing one paper's numbers into another's condition column.
- Dropping contradictory results.
