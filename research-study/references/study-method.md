# Study: Method Explanation

## Purpose

Explain a method or algorithm clearly, with assumptions, failure modes, and alternatives.

## Workflow

1. State the problem it solves.
2. Describe the core idea.
3. Explain the key steps.
4. Discuss assumptions, failure modes, and alternatives.
5. Provide a minimal example.

## Quality gates

- The problem is clearly stated.
- The core idea is explained in one sentence.
- Key steps are numbered and sequential.
- Assumptions and failure modes are explicit.
- Alternatives are mentioned, not ignored.

## Common pitfalls

- Treating the method as a black box.
- Skipping complexity analysis.
- Ignoring numerical stability.
- Confusing training and inference.
- Hiding hyperparameter sensitivity.
