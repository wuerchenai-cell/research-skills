# Study: Derivation

## Purpose

Reconstruct a formula or theorem step by step, with assumptions, boundary conditions, and limiting cases.

## Workflow

1. State the goal and known conditions.
2. List assumptions and constraints.
3. Reconstruct the derivation step by step.
4. Check units, boundary conditions, and limiting cases.
5. Provide a numeric example if useful.

## Quality gates

- Each step is justified.
- Assumptions are explicit.
- Units are consistent.
- Limiting cases are checked.
- The final result is verifiable.

## Common pitfalls

- Skipping algebraic steps.
- Hiding assumptions inside equations.
- Confusing necessary and sufficient conditions.
- Ignoring domain restrictions.
- Treating approximations as exact results.
