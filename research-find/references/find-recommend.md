# Find: Recommendation

## Purpose

Give a recommendation reason for each candidate.

## Workflow

1. Tie the recommendation to the user's goal (introduction, method, survey, baseline, etc.).
2. State why this candidate fits: scope, level, method, or access.
3. State what it does not cover.
4. Mark the confidence of the recommendation.

## Quality gates

- Each recommendation has a reason, not just a title.
- The reason connects to the user's stated goal.
- Limits and gaps are stated.

## Common pitfalls

- Ranking by prestige instead of fit.
- Listing candidates without a reading order.
